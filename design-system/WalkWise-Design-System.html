Design System

Tokens and components for the WalkWise app, built from the WalkWise Brand Identity guide and the app screens. Safety comes before speed, and that applies to the interface too: calm, high contrast, and readable at a glance while you walk.

Brand

Use the white logo on navy and the color logo on white. Never recolor, stretch, outline or re-type the logo. Candara is used only for the logotype, which ships as an image.

Color

Use the three primary colors most of the time. The tints, neutrals and status colors are extensions for UI states, so use them sparingly.

Components

Each component is shown with its class names. Tiles and tabs are interactive.

Button .ww-button

Variants: --primary --accent --secondary --ghost. Sizes: --sm 40px, --md 50px, --lg 58px. Primary (navy) is the default call to action. Keep Accent (red) for safety actions like reporting or SOS; it switches to 19px Bold for contrast. Use one primary button per view.

Icon button .ww-icon-button

The 75px red FAB is the report button. Keep it on the map on every screen, and move it up when a sheet opens.

Guidelines

  • Glanceable first. People use WalkWise while walking. Keep primary text at 20px or larger on navigation screens, and keep tap targets at least 44px.
  • Red means safety. Light Red is for the mascot, alerts and safety actions. Don't use it for decoration or for ordinary calls to action.
  • Contrast. White on navy passes WCAG AAA. White on red is about 3.5:1, so use it only for large text (19px Bold or 24px Regular and up) and icons, never for small body text.
  • Never rely on color alone. Pair every status color with an icon or a text label.
  • Soft shapes. Cards and sheets use the 35px radius, tiles use 25px, and controls are pill-shaped.
  • Tone of voice. Supportive and calm, never alarming: "Poor lighting reported ahead," not "DANGER."
  • Extensions. Tints, neutrals, status colors and the spacing scale aren't in the brand guide. Confirm them before using them in print.
